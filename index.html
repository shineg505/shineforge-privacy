ShineForge Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

ShineForge AI Company (“ShineForge”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is an AI-enabled operating system/project used to coordinate human principals and AI agents across communication channels such as WhatsApp, WeCom, email, and future supported channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, processed, stored, and protected when people communicate with ShineForge or use services connected to ShineForge.

1. Information We May Process

  • Contact and account information, such as a phone number, display name, email address, or communication-platform identifier.
  • Message content intentionally sent to ShineForge, including text and, where supported, files, images, audio, or other attachments.
  • Conversation and task context required to understand requests and maintain continuity.
  • Operational records such as timestamps, message identifiers, routing information, delivery status, error logs, audit records, approval records, and security events.
  • Work product created through the service, such as documents, plans, reports, reviews, project files, and other task outputs.
  • Technical information needed to operate integrations, authenticate requests, prevent duplicate processing, diagnose failures, and protect the service.

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for providing or securing the service.

2. How We Use Information

  • Receive, route, and respond to messages.
  • Identify the relevant human principal, AI agent, project, or conversation.
  • Provide AI-assisted analysis, drafting, planning, coordination, and task execution.
  • Maintain conversation continuity and organizational knowledge where configured.
  • Apply authorization, approval, governance, and audit controls.
  • Operate, secure, monitor, debug, and improve service reliability.
  • Detect duplicate events, misuse, technical failures, or unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for third-party advertising.

3. AI Processing

ShineForge uses AI agents and supporting software to process user requests. The current runtime may include Hermes and model services provided by OpenAI.

Information included in a request may be sent to an AI model provider when needed to perform the requested task. The amount of information provided is intended to be limited to what is reasonably necessary for that task and the configured context.

AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate. Where actions are consequential, ShineForge may apply approval, permit, authority, audit, or other governance controls before execution.

4. Communication Platforms and Service Providers

Depending on the channel or configuration, service providers may include Meta / WhatsApp, Tencent / WeCom, OpenAI, GitHub, Cloudflare, and other infrastructure, storage, email, or integration providers added in the future. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. WhatsApp Communications

  • WhatsApp and Meta may process message and account information as part of operating the WhatsApp service.
  • ShineForge may receive a WhatsApp identifier or phone number, message content, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery status, and related webhook information.
  • Messages may be routed to a configured ShineForge AI agent for processing.
  • A communication trigger such as #AI may be required before a message is sent to an AI agent, depending on the configured policy.
  • Communication access alone does not grant approval, execution, or administrative authority.

6. WeCom and Other Communication Channels

When a user communicates through WeCom, email, or another supported channel, the relevant platform may process information according to its own terms. ShineForge may apply channel-specific policies.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for operational continuity, security, auditability, governance, backup, or legal obligations. Different records may have different retention periods.

  • Short-lived technical and duplicate-detection records may be retained for a limited period.
  • Conversation or task context may be retained while useful for the relevant work.
  • Governance, approval, security, and audit evidence may be retained longer where necessary to preserve accountability.
  • Backups may persist for a limited period after primary data is changed or deleted.

8. Security

ShineForge uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including restricted credentials, authenticated and encrypted API communications, HTTPS, webhook verification, access controls, identity mapping, channel-specific trigger policies, governance controls, logging, monitoring, health checks, and audit evidence.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Sharing

ShineForge may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, process requested tasks, secure the system, or meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not provide it to data brokers for advertising purposes.

10. International Processing

Some service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. Where this occurs, information may be subject to the laws applicable in those locations and to the provider’s contractual and technical safeguards.

11. User Choices and Privacy Requests

A user may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by ShineForge, subject to technical, legal, security, audit, and operational requirements.

Until a dedicated privacy mailbox or website is established, privacy requests may be made through the same ShineForge communication channel through which the user interacts with the service, with a clear request such as “Privacy request” or “Data deletion request.” We may need to verify the requester’s identity before acting on a request.

12. Children’s Privacy

ShineForge is not designed for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. Where a higher minimum age applies under local law or the terms of a connected service, that higher minimum age should be followed.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, integrations, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

ShineForge is currently operated as an evolving AI-company project rather than through a dedicated public website.

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact ShineForge through the same communication channel used to interact with the service and clearly label the request as a privacy request.

This policy is intended to describe ShineForge’s current operating practices. It is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
